Wedding band and DJ package: what to actually book

A wedding band and DJ package usually means one thing: the live band for the evening, plus a DJ to keep the floor going after the band finishes. Here's what that after-band DJ is, when the handover happens, what it costs, and what's included with a MusicAngel package.

The short answer

Every MusicAngel evening band package already includes DJ-style music between and after the band's live sets, played through the same PA. That covers the music gap before the band starts, during their break, and from the end of the band's final set through to the venue's curfew.

For most Irish weddings that's all the "DJ" you need. Adding a separate dedicated wedding DJ is meaningful only in specific scenarios, covered below.

What's included with the band

  • Pre-band music: curated playlist while guests are taking seats, between speeches and the band starting.
  • Between-set DJ music, keeps the floor warm during the band's break.
  • Post-band music, from the end of the band's final set through to the curfew.
  • Same PA system: sound is consistent; no audible handover.
  • Curated to your taste: share preferences in your enquiry and we'll match the vibe.

The after-band DJ: what happens when the band finishes

"After-band DJ" is the shorthand couples use for the DJ who takes over the dance floor after the live band's final set. It's the single most-searched way to describe this part of a wedding, so let's be precise about what it means in practice.

At a typical Irish wedding the running order looks like this:

  • Band set one (60 minutes) — the opening set, usually starting after the meal and speeches, typically 9pm-10pm.
  • Break — DJ-style music keeps the floor warm for 20-30 minutes while the band resets.
  • Band set two (60 minutes) — the big set of the night, typically 10:30pm-11:30pm or 11pm-midnight.
  • The handover — the band's final song segues straight into the after-band DJ's first track, typically around 11:30pm-midnight.
  • After-band DJ (1-3 hours) — requests, club classics and late-night floor-fillers run through to the venue's curfew, usually 1:30am, 2am or 3am depending on the licence.

Is the after-band DJ included, or does it cost extra?

There are two ways to fill the after-band slot, and the distinction matters for your budget:

  • Included: every MusicAngel evening package already includes DJ-style music after the band's final set, played through the same PA the band used, all the way to curfew. No extra cost, no extra supplier, no gap in the music.
  • Paid extra: if you specifically want a dedicated DJ in that slot — a separate person mixing live, taking requests and working the floor — that's an add-on, typically €400-€800, and we coordinate the handover timing so the band's last song flows into their first track.

Why a gapless band-to-DJ handover matters

The ten minutes after a band's final song is when dance floors empty fastest. Guests who drift off during dead air often don't come back — they find the bar, or the conversation, and the room thins out. A gapless handover, where the band's closing number overlaps into the DJ's opening track, holds the energy and the crowd in the room. It's the difference between a wedding that ends with a full dance floor and one that quietly winds down. Every MusicAngel booking, whether the after-band slot is the included DJ music or a separate DJ, plans for that overlap ahead of the day.

When a separate DJ makes sense

There are real cases where a dedicated DJ on top of the band is worth the extra spend:

  • Late-night extension to 3am+. If your venue allows late curfew and you want a DJ pulling crowd requests for 2+ hours after the band, hire a real DJ.
  • Specific genre preferences. If you want a dedicated Afrobeats, House, or Trad-Irish DJ for a specific portion of the night.
  • Very large weddings (250+). A separate DJ can run a second dance space.
  • Specific cultural elements. For example, an Indian wedding with a sangeet DJ, or a Brazilian wedding with a samba DJ.

For everything else, the band's included DJ music is sufficient. You're paying for live performance, and the band's playlist between sets is designed to lead into and out of their own live set without disruption.

How the handover works

If you do book both a MusicAngel band and a separate DJ, we coordinate timings ahead of the day:

  • The band's final song segues into the DJ's first track without an audible gap.
  • Sound levels are matched so guests don't notice a volume jump.
  • Lighting hands off from band-stage to DJ-floor cleanly.
  • Typically the band ends 11:30pm-12am and the DJ runs the floor to 1:30am-3am.

What to ask in your enquiry

  • Confirm what's included in the band's DJ-style music slots.
  • Mention any specific genres or playlists you want covered between sets.
  • If your venue allows late curfew and you'd consider a separate DJ, ask for a quote with and without.
